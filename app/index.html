Azure cost governance

This website is a minimal workload that exists to demonstrate the cost governance around it:

  • Mandatory tagging via Azure Policy (deny without costCenter)
  • Monthly budget with alerts at 80/100/110%
  • Daily cost export to Storage + Grafana dashboard
  • Automatic scale-down of the plan to Free outside business hours

Everything deployed with Terraform.
